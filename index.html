About

A student building from the ground up

My name is Joel Talemwa. I’m currently taking WDD 130: Web Fundamentals at BYU–Idaho, where this page serves as my first real project — a place to practice semantic HTML, thoughtful CSS, and the small details that make a page feel considered rather than just functional.

Every project on this site is built by hand, without frameworks, so I can understand exactly how each piece works before reaching for anything more advanced. It’s a work in progress, the same way learning to build for the web always is.

The Pearl of Africa

Uganda

Uganda is a country in East Africa known for its rich culture, wildlife, and landscape — often called the Pearl of Africa, a name first given by Winston Churchill after he was struck by its scenery. From the shores of Lake Victoria to the forests that shelter mountain gorillas, it’s a place built on color and life.
